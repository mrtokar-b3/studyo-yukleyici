Studyo Yukleyici

Studyo Yukleyici is a private desktop tool. Its only user is the owner of two YouTube channels, who runs it on their own computer to upload and schedule their own videos, set thumbnails, check video details and read the channels' analytics through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API.

It is not offered to the public, has no accounts or sign-ups, and does not collect data from anyone else.
